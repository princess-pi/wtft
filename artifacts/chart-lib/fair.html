Categories the booth rides on

  
The chart has 14 categories, and any other value throws. The booth's items borrow a slot each. In the first picture an item takes its slot's colour and place in the stack; in the second the booth's words.categories sets both, passing each slot's own colour in the chart's order, so the bars match. Cost bucket mode draws no stack: each slot with sales gets one █, placed by its revenue, and where two land on the same cell the larger takes it, or on a tie the one earlier in the stack, and the other draws nothing. The table lists Souvenirs whether or not the booth sells them; the booth uses other only when souvenirs are ticked.

  


  

    The call
