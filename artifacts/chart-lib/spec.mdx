# Chart library spec

A library is only a library once someone has reused it. The chart has one user, wtft. This page is the second: a booth at the state fair, sold by item and by time of day, drawn by wtft's own `buildWtftLines`, which takes the booth's words as options. The page runs it inside `withTerminal` (`artifacts/renderer/report.ts`), which stands in for the terminal's width, and in the browser for `process`. The page passes it no clock, so the timeline strip reads the browser's. Where the chart won't do what the booth needs, that's a finding.

The fair page draws the booth twice, both times through `buildWtftLines`. The first picture is what the chart returns with no words. The second is the same call without the session name and with the booth's own `words`: a title, a legend of the booth's items, units, a divider, a key, a footer and cache line switched off, and a warning. The table under the pictures lists each word the booth passes and how many lines of the first picture carried wtft's. The timeline strip (F2) stays in the second picture, because it stays on wtft's title line.

## The booth

One sale is one interaction. The chart reads these fields of the interaction, and `sessionNameSuffix` from its options. It also merges duplicates by `messageId`, and an interrupted copy makes the sale `interrupted` whatever its `_cat`. It reads `model`, `serverToolCost`, and the other token counts: `outputTokens`, `cacheReadTokens`, `cacheWriteTokens`, `reasoningTokens`. With `_cat` unset it classifies from `interrupted`, `files`, `toolCats`, `commands`, `texts` and `unrecognizedTool`. The booth sets `_cat`, leaves `model`, `messageId`, `interrupted`, `toolCats` and `unrecognizedTool` unset, and zeroes or empties the rest.

| Booth | Chart field | Note |
|---|---|---|
| when the sale rang up | `timestamp` | a bin is minutes, hours, days, weeks, or a count of sales |
| revenue | `cost` | printed with `$`: the booth passes no `words.currency`, which would replace it |
| units sold | `inputTokens` | the chart's second measure is tokens: input, output, cache read, cache write and reasoning added. The booth sets only `inputTokens`, which also feeds the CH line's total ops |
| item | `_cat` | one of the chart's 14 categories |
| a lunch or dinner sale, billed 1.5 times | `surgePriced` | one such sale marks its whole bin: the time label and number columns turn bold orange, and a `⚡` (`!!` under `--no-emoji`) fills a gap when it is 2 wide. The bar keeps its category colours |
| the power went out | `cacheMiss` | a divider under that row: `Cache Miss` with no words, `Power cut` with the booth's |
| the booth's name | `sessionNameSuffix` | an option of `buildWtftLines`, not a field of the sale. `.jsonl` is stripped and `...` plus the last 4 characters print. Optional |

Six items ride on six of the chart's categories: lattes, apples, funnel cake, pie, corn dogs, lemonade. Souvenirs, when ticked, ride on `other`. With no `words` the stack follows the chart's own category order. With `words.categories` it follows that list, and any slot left out of the list stacks after the listed ones. The booth lists its items in the chart's order, so both pictures stack alike.

## What worked without a change

- A stack per bin, coloured by category: a running total in cumulative mode, and in bucket mode with units the bin's own amount. Bucket mode with revenue draws no stack: each category with sales gets one `█`, placed by its own cost, and where two land on the same cell the larger cost takes it, or on a tie the one earlier in the stack order, and the other draws nothing.
- `█` for earlier bins against `✨` for this bin, for revenue and for units sold, in cumulative mode only, by the chart spec's Encoding law: `✨` is two cells, sits after its category's `█`, and each one becomes `**` under `--no-emoji`, which the booth never passes. Bucket mode draws no `✨`.
- Four number columns. The label area compacts, in five steps, once it passes a quarter of the width, which shortens the number columns and the gap, never the bin label, so long labels compact the rest. Under 15 bar cells the chart drops the units pair, then the revenue pair, whatever the caller asked for.
- Bins of minutes, hours, days, weeks, or a count of sales. `-i 25t` is every 25 sales. A week bin ignores its size, so `2w` bins like `1w`. Multi-day bins restart at each month boundary.
- A date line where the day changes, in any timezone. An invalid zone falls back to the host's local time without a message. A scale line with ticks, dropped when the bar is under 15 cells or a units chart's scale is 0. A revenue scale of 0 becomes $1.
- Rows for a surge-priced bin in bold orange. The chart's field is `surgePriced`, and no row says "surge". The only SURGE text is the title badge, which comes from the model's price schedule and has nothing to do with `surgePriced`. The booth bills lunch and dinner at 1.5 times and marks those sales `surgePriced`: 12:00, 13:00, 18:00 and 19:00.
- Padding rows out to the limit, at most 1000.

## What the chart can't say

- **Time of day across days.** F16. An hourly bin is a date and an hour. Lunch on three days is three separate rows.
- **A bar per item.** The row axis is time, never a category.
- **Anything but two measures.** The columns are one currency and one count. A booth that sells by weight has no third.
- **A second divider.** A caller renames the one divider with `words.cacheMissLabel`, as the booth's power cut does, but cannot add another: the rules between rows are that divider, set by `cacheMiss`, and the date.

## Decisions

- **Q-A.** One chart with caller-supplied words and categories. wtft becomes its first caller, and its 14 categories are the default list. This settles F1, F3, F5 to F7, F10 and F11 as caller words and the category list, F8 and F9 as switches, and F4 only as a relabelling of the 14 slots.
- **Q-B.** The timeline strip stays on wtft's title line. The library is to take a header line from its caller, and the same work carries F12 to F14: width in, clock in, and the chart importable without the parser. None of it has landed: `docs/spec-398-chart-words.md` lists it as later work.
- **Q-C.** The option-to-chart function is `chartLines`, whichever way Q-A goes.
- **Q-D.** Time of day across days is out of scope. F16 stays standing.
- **Q-E.** The turn-stride lines the old chart picker offered, every 10 and every 100 turns, stay gone. The chart never drew them.
